Control your computer without touching it.

Pipeline

Three steps between your hand and your OS — all of them on-device.

01 / capture

Read the frame

Your webcam feeds ZeroTouch at 60fps. No footage is stored or uploaded — frames are discarded the instant they're processed.

02 / interpret

Map the landmarks

A lightweight on-device model tracks 21 points per hand and classifies the pose against your gesture library in real time.

03 / act

Trigger the action

Matched gestures fire native OS events — media keys, window snapping, scroll, custom shortcuts — through a signed system driver.
